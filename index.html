  • Homemade menemen

    Two eggs folded through a fresh tomato sauce with mozzarella, served with caramelised Turkish bread. The one to order if you want breakfast to taste of somewhere.

  • Egg & avocado toast

    Two poached eggs on sourdough with crushed avocado and a mixed salad. The quiet, reliable one — and the plate it arrives on is half the reason people photograph it.

  • French toast

    Brioche dipped in cinnamon-vanilla batter, stacked with fresh fruit, cream, butter and maple syrup or Nutella. Order it for the table and pretend you are sharing.

  • Iced latte

    Espresso poured long over milk and ice. There is a full coffee list behind it — flat white, cortado, Turkish coffee, matcha — plus frappés and shakes when it is warm.

The café

A place to pause. A reason to return.

Hackberries is a neighbourhood café on Windmill Lane. It is the kind of room where a quick coffee turns into an hour, and nobody minds.

Marble tables, velvet chairs in green and blue, rattan pendants and a wall of foliage and white flowers against exposed brick. Bright enough to work in, soft enough to stay in.
